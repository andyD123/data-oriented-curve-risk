## Live QuantLib results

QuantLib 1.43 repriced real OvernightIndexedCoupon amounts through
relinked DiscountCurve objects under Hagan instantaneous-forward box
bumps. The discount/forecast curve is the SAME and the coupon is fully
forecast. This is curve-wave sensitivity, **not** stochastic-model
convexity adjustment or quote-rebootstrap gamma.

| Quantity | Standard coupon | Deliberately stressed coupon |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accrual start | 2027-01-04 | 2047-01-07 |
| Accrual end | 2027-04-06 | 2048-01-06 |
| Payment date | 2027-04-08 | 2048-04-08 |
| Lag, calendar days | 2 | 93 |
| Base PV — QuantLib and formula | +8711.821390562 | +16735.955802027 |
| Frozen-weight cross-gamma | +12.032090 | +4252.547013 |
| Rank-two lag correction | -1369.910822 | -123973.780664 |
| **Correct analytic mixed gamma** | **-1357.878732688** | **-119721.233651181** |

The live mixed four-repricing finite differences agree and display
the expected O(h^2) truncation trend:

| Step h | Standard mixed FD | Stressed mixed FD |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0.008 | -1357.879653323 | -119722.586599678 |
| 0.004 | -1357.878962850 | -119721.571887396 |
| 0.002 | -1357.878790259 | -119721.318210168 |

First-order accrual and base-PV checks also passed. The lag term
is financially important: the frozen-weight approximation gets the
**sign** wrong in both cases.

A post-hoc Richardson extrapolation from the rounded output above
is within roughly 1e-7 of the analytic second derivative. Do not
treat that extrapolation as a full-precision independent witness.

## Independent research benchmark (same reported cloud host)

100,000 fixed discounted cashflows, 66 waves, seed 20261010,
8,713 central-bump portfolio valuations. GCC 13.3 / -O3 -march=native:

| Kernel | Recorded time |
|---|---:|
| Structured first-order + gamma and full Hessian | 1.027803 ms |
| Direct analytic cashflow Hessian | 41.597637 ms |
| Bump-wave overlap precomputation | 51.082877 ms |
| Reprice all finite-difference Hessian entries | 6394.291951 ms |

**40.47x** against the stated direct analytic method; **6271.02x**
against the stated full finite-difference baseline including overlap
precomputation. Max relative structured/direct Hessian error:
1.57398883e-14. Central finite-difference error: 1.54918211e-06.

This is an **aggregated fixed-cashflow** Hessian on this hardware,
not a best-in-class AAD, general QuantLib Hessian, production
500,000-instrument mixed-book, or per-instrument dense-Hessian comparison.

## Limitations / future validation

The live reconciliation supports ONE selected off-diagonal pair
(accrual wave versus lag-only wave) in two real coupon setups.
The coupon dates in the current runner coincide with wave boundaries.
A further independent test should put the accrual end INSIDE a
wave and exercise a genuinely nonzero lag-related diagonal correction
(-2*A*r_k*s_k), and ideally compare the complete local Hessian.

No claim is made for already-fixed coupons, observation shifts,
lockouts, compounded spreads, caps/floors, HJM stochastic settlement
convexity or quote-level curve reconstruction.

Prior statements saying live QuantLib has never run applied at
initial authoring time but are **superseded by the externally reported
11 October 2026 QuantLib 1.43 execution**, for these two scenarios.
